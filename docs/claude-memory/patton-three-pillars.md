Patton Motors is **three things at once**, and the live site communicates only
one of them:

1. **Dealer** — 40 collector cars on the floor, buy / sell / trade / consign.
2. **Auction house** — being added. Simulated with real stock for now.
3. **A lounge** — black walls with gold cornice, white Chesterfields, a gold
   monkey on a rope, a white deer head, car art in frames, a racing game on a
   big screen, a full bar and an espresso machine. Open Saturdays 9–1 for
   "Cars, Coffee, Cocktails and Conversation".

**The single best line in the whole business is on the About page and appears
nowhere on the homepage:** *"PATTON MOTORS — A BOHEMIAN CAR PLACE."* It is the
one sentence no competitor can say, and it covers all three pillars at once.

Other facts the homepage never uses, all from `/about/` and the blog:

- There is an **art studio inside** — car artists make paintings, sculptures,
  3D-printed cars and personalised slot-car sets on site.
- The **logo was drawn by Dave Kindig** of Kindig Design.
- John and Edison Sarkisyan build cars that regularly place at SEMA.
- Exclusive US dealer for **S-Klub**, Los Angeles.
- Between Palm Beach and Miami — "one of the largest car cultures in the USA".
- A Chiron sits feet from a 997 GT3 RS and a 1-of-1 LaFerrari that belonged to
  Sammy Hagar.
- Founded by Martin Patton; his wife Brenda is named in the copy too.
