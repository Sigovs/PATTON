Sell & consign

Three ways out of your garage

Tell us what you have and we will tell you which of the three gets you the most for it — including when the answer is none of them.

01

Outright

We buy it today, with our own money. The fastest route, and you trade a little of the number for the certainty.

02

Consignment

It sits on our floor, insured, detailed and photographed properly, and we sell it as one of ours. You set the reserve.

03

A lot

It goes on the block with a reserve and a date. Best for the cars where the price is an argument rather than a listing.
